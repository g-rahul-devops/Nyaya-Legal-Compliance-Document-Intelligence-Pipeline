MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT (MSA)
NCS Telco+ and Client Services

===== 1. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY =====

1.1 Liability Cap
Neither party shall be liable to the other for any damages exceeding twelve (12) 
months of fees actually paid by Client in the twelve (12) months preceding the 
event giving rise to liability, except as provided in Section 1.2.

1.2 Exceptions to Cap
The liability cap in Section 1.1 shall not apply to:
(a) Either party's indemnification obligations under Section 2
(b) Breach of confidentiality obligations
(c) Either party's gross negligence or willful misconduct
(d) Infringement of intellectual property rights

===== 2. INDEMNIFICATION =====

2.1 Indemnification by NCS Telco+
NCS Telco+ shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Client from and against 
any third-party claims, damages, and costs (including reasonable attorneys' fees) 
arising out of:
(a) NCS Telco+'s breach of this Agreement
(b) NCS Telco+'s infringement or misappropriation of any intellectual property right
(c) NCS Telco+'s material breach of its representations and warranties

2.2 Indemnification by Client
Client shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless NCS Telco+ from any third-party 
claims arising out of Client's use of the Services in violation of this Agreement 
or applicable law.

2.3 Indemnification Triggers
An indemnification obligation is triggered immediately upon written notice from 
the indemnified party, provided that the indemnifying party retains control of 
the defense. Failure to provide prompt notice shall not relieve the indemnifying 
party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced.

===== 3. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION =====

3.1 Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws 
of India, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

3.2 Jurisdiction
Subject to Section 3.3, the exclusive jurisdiction and venue for any legal 
action or proceeding relating to this Agreement shall be the courts of Mumbai, 
India.

3.3 Arbitration
Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall first be subject to mediation 
in Mumbai. If mediation fails, disputes shall be resolved by arbitration under 
the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, administered by the Indian Council 
of Arbitration (ICA).

===== 4. TERM AND TERMINATION =====

4.1 Initial Term
This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial 
term of three (3) years unless earlier terminated.

4.2 Renewal
Upon expiration of the initial term, this Agreement shall automatically renew 
for successive one (1) year periods unless either party provides written notice 
of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before expiration.

4.3 Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if:
(a) The other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within 
    thirty (30) days of written notice
(b) The other party becomes insolvent or bankrupt

===== 5. CONFIDENTIALITY =====

5.1 Definition of Confidential Information
Confidential Information means all non-public, proprietary information disclosed 
by one party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including but not 
limited to business plans, technical data, customer lists, and financial 
information.

5.2 Obligations
Each party shall: (a) protect Confidential Information using reasonable security 
measures; (b) limit access to employees with a legitimate business need; 
(c) not disclose to third parties without prior written consent.

5.3 Exceptions
Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that: (a) is publicly 
available; (b) was independently developed; (c) is required to be disclosed by law.

END OF DOCUMENT
