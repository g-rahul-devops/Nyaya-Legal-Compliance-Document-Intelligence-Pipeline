REGULATORY COMPLIANCE CIRCULAR
Document ID: RFC-2024-Q3
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: July 1, 2024

From: Securities and Telecom Regulatory Commission (STRC)
To: All Licensed Telecom Service Providers
Date: June 15, 2024

RE: Mandatory Quarterly Compliance Reporting Requirement

Dear Licensee,

This circular sets forth mandatory compliance reporting requirements for all 
telecom service providers operating under our jurisdiction.

===== 1. QUARTERLY COMPLIANCE REPORT REQUIREMENT =====

1.1 Submission Mandate
Every licensed telecom service provider must submit a comprehensive Quarterly 
Compliance Report (QCR) to the Securities and Telecom Regulatory Commission 
(STRC) by the 15th day of the month following each fiscal quarter:

- Q1 (Jan-Mar): Submit by April 15th
- Q2 (Apr-Jun): Submit by July 15th  <<< NEXT DEADLINE: July 15, 2024
- Q3 (Jul-Sep): Submit by October 15th
- Q4 (Oct-Dec): Submit by January 15th (following year)

1.2 Content Requirements
Each QCR must include:

(a) Network Compliance Status - uptime percentage, incident count by severity, 
    network outage durations exceeding 15 minutes
    
(b) Service Quality Metrics - average call drop rate, network congestion hours, 
    customer complaint resolution time
    
(c) Regulatory Findings - any compliance violations identified internally, 
    status of remediation efforts, audit findings from the previous quarter
    
(d) Personnel Changes - any changes to Chief Compliance Officer, General Counsel, 
    or Chief Technology Officer positions
    
(e) Infrastructure Investments - capital expenditure on network upgrades, 
    security systems, and disaster recovery
    
(f) Incident Log - all critical security incidents, data breaches, or customer 
    data access anomalies

1.3 Submission Method
Reports must be submitted electronically via the STRC Compliance Portal 
(https://compliance.strc.gov.in) using the QCR XML schema v2.1. Hard copies 
are optional but recommended for audit trail purposes.

1.4 Certification
The QCR must be signed and certified by the Chief Compliance Officer (or 
General Counsel if no dedicated CCO) affirming that:
- The information is accurate and complete to the best of their knowledge
- Internal controls have been in place during the reporting period
- All identified violations have been disclosed

===== 2. PENALTIES FOR NON-COMPLIANCE =====

2.1 Late Submission Penalties
- Submission 1-5 days late: Administrative fine of INR 50,000 per day
- Submission 6-15 days late: Administrative fine of INR 100,000 per day
- Submission more than 15 days late: Administrative fine of INR 500,000 plus 
  show-cause notice for license suspension

2.2 Incomplete Submission
If a QCR is found to be incomplete or materially inaccurate:
- First instance: Notice of deficiency; 7 days to resubmit. Fine of INR 100,000.
- Second instance within 12 months: Fine of INR 250,000 and formal audit.
- Third instance within 24 months: Fine of INR 500,000 and license review.

2.3 False Certification
If the signing officer knowingly certifies false information:
- Civil penalty: INR 5,000,000 or 5 years imprisonment, or both
- License suspension for 6 months to 2 years
- Mandatory replacement of Chief Compliance Officer

2.4 Recurring Violation Pattern
If a licensee fails to submit three consecutive QCRs on time, or submits three 
deficient reports in a 24-month period, the STRC shall initiate formal 
enforcement proceedings leading to potential license revocation.

===== 3. EXEMPTIONS AND SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCES =====

3.1 Force Majeure
Licensees experiencing genuine force majeure events (natural disasters, government 
intervention, state of emergency) may request a one-time extension of up to 
14 days. Request must be filed within 48 hours of the event with supporting 
documentation.

3.2 Bankruptcy or Restructuring
Licensees undergoing court-ordered restructuring or bankruptcy may request a 
deferment of QCR submission. Deferment requests must be filed with court orders 
attached; STRC will grant deferment of not exceeding 45 days.

===== 4. EFFECTIVE DATE =====

This circular takes effect immediately for all QCRs due on or after July 15, 
2024. Licensees are advised to implement necessary systems and processes 
without delay.
